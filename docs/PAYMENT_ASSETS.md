# Payment acceptance artwork

Original SVG artwork is served locally from `public/payments` and `vercel-app/public/payments`. The artwork is not a button or a claim of bank approval. Wallet availability is explained beside it.

Sources retrieved on 2026-10-20:

- Visa: official Visa Product Design System, https://design.visa.com/ (the `v-logo` SVG).
- Mastercard: https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/mccom/shared/header/ma_symbol.svg
- Apple Pay: https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/Apple-Pay-Mark.zip (`Apple Pay Mark/SVG/Apple_Pay_Mark_RGB_041619.svg`).
- Google Pay: https://developers.google.com/static/pay/api/download-assets/Google-Pay-Acceptance.zip (`Google Pay Mark/google-pay-mark_800.svg`).

Marks remain the property of their respective owners. Preserve original proportions, colors and clear space. Payment options shown on the acquirer's hosted page determine actual availability.
